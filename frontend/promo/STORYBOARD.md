# Promo video: storyboard

Audience: the Meshtastic community in Norway, on Facebook and Discord.
About 77 s, 16:9, no sound (music is added in post), Norwegian captions.
The real app against production, real node names. Pings are live traffic, sped up about 12×.
A drawn pointer shows every click (a touch marker on the phone); panels are shown zoomed in.
Recorded by `takes/map.spec.ts`, then `takes/phone.spec.ts` (README.md).
